USD is the **daily band/parity currency** in these band records. A basket reference does not establish a fixed basket peg or known weights. A wider permitted band does not prove that realized flexibility immediately increased.

**Verified EUR-linked arrangement: DKK**

[The ECB's release dated 31 December 1998](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/1998/html/pr981231_3.en.html) publishes the ERM II central rate and intervention limits, effective 1 January 1999: **DKK 7.46038 per EUR**, upper/lower intervention rates 7.62824 and 7.29252 (approximately +/-2.25%). [Danmarks Nationalbank's participation note](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/en/news-and-knowledge/publications-and-speeches/archive-publications/1999/denmarks-participation-in-erm-ii) explains that participation had been agreed at the September 1998 ECOFIN meeting. Therefore, 31 December is the verified public date of the exact parameters, not a claim that participation itself was a surprise then.

The [central bank's policy explanation](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/en/frequently-asked-questions/questions-regarding-fixed-exchange-rate-policy) confirms the EUR anchor. This is an EUR band arrangement, distinct from a USD peg and from CHF's former one-sided EUR floor. Retain DKK in the existing forecasting universe. Do not backcast a live EUR series before its launch.

- Start with official central-bank announcements and institutional histories. Record the actual publication date, rather than the date a retrospective history describes.
- For arrangements, include anchor and flexibility separately. Record an announced future effective date separately from the announcement date.
- For intervention, distinguish the action date from public confirmation and the later release of amounts. Missing disclosure does not establish no intervention.
- For IMF/debt events, record approval/default/restructuring announcements separately; do not backdate later outcomes or annual classifications.
- A date alone does not establish causation. Write the proposed mechanism and record conflicting evidence when adding the event to a correlation timeline.
- When a source is retrospective and its original public date cannot be established, mark it as descriptive evidence. It cannot yet supply an origin-time forecast feature.
